# In The Sky Fire Legacy v3.0

- Sky View restores local Moon, Sun and planet positions alongside bright stars.
- Satellite selection tries Wikipedia imagery, then NASA Images, before retaining the generated schematic fallback.
- Weather history cards are shorter with wider uniform plot areas, larger local-condition text and corrected alert-panel fit.
- Flight route lines use sampled great-circle arcs instead of straight screen-space lines.
- Panels are more transparent so the star/nebula backdrop is easier to see.
