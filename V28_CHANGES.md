# v2.8

- Flight tracking no longer depends on successful route lookup; live ADS-B position remains active.
- Flight camera now uses Mercator latitude to match the bundled world map and shows a flashing acquisition box before the smooth follow zoom.
- Cached ADSBDB route data is used when live route lookup fails.
- World Clocks collapses the full instrument body by default.
- On This Day is filtered to aviation, aerospace and astronomy topics, with local fallback content.
- Sky View removes the unused action/control stack and expands the live sky and selected-object/reference-image areas.
- Sky View layer switches moved to Settings.
- Satellite hierarchy: SatNOGS TLE primary, CelesTrak stations/visual fallback, cached provider responses.
- Global panels are more translucent and the star/nebula background is brighter and denser.
